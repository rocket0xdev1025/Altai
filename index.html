How to Buy

Four moves from an empty wallet to $Altai. You spend ETH on Robinhood Chain.

  1. 01

    Connect on Robinhood Chain

    Open Robinhood Wallet, MetaMask, or any EVM wallet and switch to Robinhood Chain. The chain ID is 4663. Robinhood Wallet already lists the network.

  2. 02

    Bring Robinhood ETH

    Bridge ETH onto Robinhood Chain from Uniswap, or through the Arbitrum portal with Robinhood Chain selected. That ETH is what you swap. A little of it also pays gas.

  3. 03

    Open the $Altai swap

    Press Buy Now. Uniswap opens on Robinhood Chain with $Altai as the token you receive. Match it against the contract copied from this page.

  4. 04

    Confirm the trade

    Enter the Robinhood ETH you want to spend, read the quote and the price impact, then confirm in your wallet. Leave a pinch of ETH so the transaction can clear.
